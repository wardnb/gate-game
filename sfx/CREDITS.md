## Service songs (`song_<branch>.mp3`)

Recordings by U.S. military bands are works of the U.S. government and are in the **public domain**; the compositions
used are public domain as well. Copies taken from Wikimedia Commons, trimmed, faded at each end and loudness-normalised.

| File | Piece | Performed by | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `song_army.mp3` | The Army Goes Rolling Along (Gruber, arr. Sousa) | U.S. Army Band | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Army_Goes_Rolling_Along_-_U.S._Army_Band.ogg |
| `song_marines.mp3` | The Marines' Hymn | U.S. Marine Band | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Marines%27_Hymn,_USMC_Band.ogg |
| `song_navy.mp3` | Anchors Aweigh (Zimmermann, 1906) | U.S. Air Force Band (1998) | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:%22Anchors_Aweigh%22,_performed_by_the_United_States_Air_Force_Band_in_1998.ogg |
| `song_coastguard.mp3` | Semper Paratus (Van Boskerck, 1927) | U.S. Coast Guard Band | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Semper_Paratus_by_USCG_Band.ogg |
| `song_airforce.mp3` | In Pursuit (MSgt Kent Baker), first 58 s | U.S. Air Force Band | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:In_Pursuit_-_Concert_Band_-_United_States_Air_Force_Band.mp3 |
| `song_spaceforce.mp3` | Breaking Barriers (MSgt John Bliss) | U.S. Air Force Band | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Breaking_Barriers_-_Concert_Band_-_United_States_Air_Force.mp3 |

The official Air Force song ("The U.S. Air Force", 1939) is still under copyright (Carl Fischer), and no public-domain
recording of the Space Force's "Semper Supra" was available, so those two branches use original marches written and
recorded by Air Force Band members.
